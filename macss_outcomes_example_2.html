MACSS Career Outcomes

Customized learning and possible career trajectories based on an individual's interests are key benefits of interdisciplinary study. Given that breadth and depth, MACSS graduates accept positions across a diversity of positions, organizations, and industries. MACSS places more graduates in data and research, in both for-profit and not-for-profit sectors, than any other field.
